## Part 1: Strategic Overview

### 1. Executive Summary
The Security Operations Center (SOC) domain is the operational command and control layer of the CYBERMIND CyberOS. It provides the collaborative workspace where analysts conduct investigations, correlate evidence, and execute decisions. It acts purely as a consumer of underlying platform services, orchestrating them into a cohesive analyst experience.

### 2. Vision & 3. Design Goals
To unify fragmented operational data into a single pane of glass, accelerating analyst investigations without duplicating the responsibilities of the SIEM, SOAR, or CTI domains.
- **Goals:** Centralized Case Management, highly collaborative War Rooms, auditable decision tracking, and 100% reliance on the platform's Trust, Intelligence, Discovery, and Execution layers.

### 4. Guiding Principles
- **Constitutional Alignment (Rule #22):** Operational domains coordinate expertise, not infrastructure. They consume shared platform services rather than reimplementing identity, intelligence, discovery, execution, or observability.
- **SOC Owns Decisions, Not Telemetry:** The SOC does not ingest logs. It triages alerts generated by the SIEM.

## Part 2: Domain-Driven Design (DDD)

### 5. Ubiquitous Language
- **Alert:** A notification of suspicious activity (managed by SIEM, referenced by SOC).
- **Case:** A container for an active investigation.
- **Evidence:** Immutable pointers to data (logs, intel, artifacts) relevant to a Case.
- **Timeline:** A chronologically ordered sequence of events and analyst actions.
- **Decision:** An auditable, human-in-the-loop operational verdict (e.g., "Declare Incident", "Mark as False Positive").

### 6. Bounded Contexts & 7. Context Map
- **Dependencies (Upstream):** 
  - *Trust:* Identity
  - *Intelligence:* AI Platform, Knowledge Graph, CTI, Search
  - *Execution:* Workflow & Automation
  - *Telemetry (Future):* SIEM
- **Consumers (Downstream):** Reporting, GRC.

### 8. Domain Model & 9. Operational Object Taxonomy
- `Case`, `Investigation`, `WarRoom`.
- `AnalystNote`, `Finding`, `Recommendation`.
- `EvidenceLink`, `DecisionRecord`, `Escalation`.

## Part 3: Architecture & Operational Lifecycle

### 10. Investigation Lifecycle
1. **Triage:** Alert received from SIEM. Analyst reviews AI summaries and CTI context.
2. **Investigation:** Case created. Evidence gathered via Search and Graph.
3. **Analysis:** Collaboration in War Room. Timeline constructed.
4. **Decision:** Analyst records a formal verdict.
5. **Containment/Recovery:** SOC triggers a SOAR playbook via the Workflow platform.
6. **Closure:** Lessons learned documented; Case archived.

### 11. Case Management Architecture & 12. Investigation Workspace
- **Case Management:** Tracks priority, severity, assignment, and status. Supports linking, merging, and splitting cases.
- **Workspace:** A React/Vite unified frontend pulling APIs from Graph (Blast Radius), CTI (Threat Actor context), and Workflow (Playbook status) into a single view.

### 13. Evidence Model & 14. Timeline Model
- **Evidence:** SOC does not store raw PCAP or logs. It stores URIs/References to the originating systems (e.g., SIEM, Storage).
- **Timeline:** Automatically populated by the system (e.g., "AI suggested playbook") and manually appended by analysts (e.g., "Analyst added note").

### 15. Collaboration Model & 16. Decision Model & 17. Escalation Model
- **Collaboration:** Real-time websockets (via Event Bus) power shared notes, @mentions, and Shift Handover summaries.
- **Decisions:** Cryptographically signed (via Identity session) to ensure non-repudiation.
- **Escalation:** Reassigns Case ownership and modifies SLA timers.

## Part 4: Integrations

### 18. Event Model
- **Published:** `CaseCreated`, `EvidenceCollected`, `CaseEscalated`, `OperationalDecisionRecorded`, `CaseClosed`.
- **Consumed:** `AlertCreated` (from SIEM), `WorkflowCompleted` (from Workflow).

### 19. AI Integration
- AI Platform provides *Investigative Assistance*: summarizes alerts, generates case timelines, and drafts Shift Handover reports. AI cannot close a case independently.

### 20. Knowledge Graph Integration
- Provides visual link-analysis within the Investigation Workspace, displaying the Attack Path and Blast Radius.

### 21. Search Integration & 22. CTI Integration
- Search retrieves historical cases and knowledge articles.
- CTI provides the authoritative threat actor context for IOCs attached to the Case.

### 23. Workflow Integration
- When an analyst clicks "Isolate Host," the SOC API publishes an event to the Workflow Platform, which executes the `HostIsolation` playbook.

### 24. Identity & 25. Notification Integration
- Identity enforces RBAC (e.g., only L3 Analysts can approve global containment).
- Notification handles @mentions and SLA breach alerts.

### 26. Public APIs
- `POST /api/soc/cases`
- `GET /api/soc/cases/{id}/timeline`
- `POST /api/soc/cases/{id}/decisions`

## Part 5: Infrastructure & Operations

### 27. Storage Abstraction
- PostgreSQL for relational operational data (Cases, Notes, Decisions). 

### 28. Security Architecture & 29. Multi-Tenant Strategy
- Enforces Need-to-Know. A Case containing TLP:RED intelligence requires specific clearance claims.
- Strict `tenant_id` isolation for Managed Security Service Provider (MSSP) environments.

### 30. Performance & 31. Scalability Strategy
- High-concurrency WebSocket servers for War Room real-time collaboration.

### 32. Disaster Recovery, 33. Monitoring, & 34. Observability
- OpenTelemetry metrics: `soc.case.triage_time`, `soc.case.resolution_time`, `soc.analyst.utilization`.

### 35. Audit Strategy & 36. Data Lifecycle
- Every Analyst Note, Case Modification, and Decision is permanently logged to an immutable audit table.
- Closed cases transition to cold storage after 1 year but remain indexed by Search.

## Part 6: Governance

### 37. Engineering Decisions & 38. ADRs
- **EDR-SOC-01:** No operational telemetry stored in SOC database; only Evidence URIs.
- **EDR-SOC-02:** Strict separation of workflow logic—SOC triggers workflows by name/event, never by executing scripts directly.

### 39. Risks & 40. Future Expansion
- **Risk:** Analyst overload. Mitigated by AI alert summarization and automated triage workflows.
- **Expansion:** Seamless integration with the forthcoming SIEM and SOAR domains.

### 41. Operational Metrics
- MTTA (Mean Time to Acknowledge), MTTR (Mean Time to Resolve), False Positive Rate, Playbook Automation Rate.
